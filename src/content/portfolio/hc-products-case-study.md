Every business needs a great website — that's your **Set Up**. HC Products came to us with a loyal local customer base and no online presence. A month later, they had a storefront working for them 24/7.

## The challenge

HC Products needed more than a pretty site. They needed a store that:

- Loads fast on phones, where most of their customers browse
- Makes ordering effortless — no account required to buy
- Lets the team update products without touching code

## What we built

We designed a mobile-first storefront with big, clear product imagery and a checkout flow with the fewest steps possible. The design system matches their brand: clean layouts, strong typography, and photos that do the selling.

> "It finally feels like our store — just online." — HC Products team

## The launch

After launch we **Leveled Up** their local visibility: product pages optimized for local search, Google Business Profile connected, and click-to-call buttons on every page.

The result: real customers finding the store, calling, and ordering — the site paid for itself in the first month.
