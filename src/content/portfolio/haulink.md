Moving freight takes more than trucks and timelines. It takes the right people, the right assignments, and a clear view of what is happening on every job. HauLink brings customers, haulers, brokers, and contractors into one connected platform.

## The challenge

HauLink needed more than a product page. It needed a digital experience that could make a multi-sided platform feel simple and useful at a glance:

- Haulers should see how the platform helps them choose work and manage their operation
- Brokers should understand how to bring projects in and fill available capacity
- Contractors should know how to track every truck and pay for completed work

## What we built

We designed a product-led experience that leads with HauLink's core promise: connecting customers to truckers. The structure gives each audience a clear entry point, then explains the value in practical terms — from driver schedules and payment terms to project tracking and fast payment options.

HauLink Pro takes the experience further by giving truckers the freedom to choose the projects they want to work on. A prominent demo path turns interest into a concrete next step without overwhelming visitors with platform jargon.

## The launch

The result is a sharper front door for the platform: one that explains the ecosystem, gives every user type a reason to keep exploring, and makes the demo request feel natural.

By putting the workflow before the feature list, HauLink can speak to the people who keep projects moving — and show them exactly where the platform fits.
