Every restaurant has a story behind the plate. For The Kabob Guy, it starts with seasoned meats, fresh sides, and the heat of the grill. We built a website that puts that story first and gives hungry guests a clear way in.

## The challenge

The Kabob Guy needed more than a digital menu. The website had to:

- Make the food look as inviting online as it does fresh off the grill
- Help visitors understand the menu at a glance
- Give first-time guests an easy way to find the restaurant and get in touch

## What we built

We designed a mobile-first restaurant experience with strong photography, generous spacing, and a visual rhythm inspired by the grill. The menu brings signature plates into focus — from beef kabob and beef lule to chicken breast, chicken lule, Greek salad, and hummus with pita chips.

The information architecture keeps the essentials close: visitors can move from the story to the menu, browse the gallery, and reach The Kabob Guy directly without having to hunt for the next step.

> "The flavor starts at the grill." — The Kabob Guy

## The result

The finished site gives The Kabob Guy a digital home with the same character as the restaurant: bold, welcoming, and focused on the food. Every page brings guests closer to the menu, the story, and the people behind the grill.

That is what a restaurant website should do: make the decision to visit feel easy, and make the food memorable before the first bite.
